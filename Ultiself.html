UltiSelf: Social media ads

Most people discover an app like Ultiself in the middle of a scroll, somewhere between a meme and a random cooking video. You get about two seconds to make them care. During my internship at Ultiself, that was my whole job: make people stop, download the app, and stick around and subscribe.

Getting an ad to work was only half the job. The other half was figuring out why it worked and squeezing more out of it. So I’d take my best performers, change one small thing (the hook, the hook text, the call to action), post the new version, and compare.

After Effects was home base. Around it I used a few AI tools to speed things up: ElevenLabs for voiceovers, Arcads for avatars, Google Flow with JSON promts for videos and Submagic for captions.

My biggest takeaway from Ultiself is that good ads are tested, not guessed. I learned to look at the numbers, stay curious about small changes, and not get attached to my first version. I also got a lot more comfortable mixing AI tools with a proper editing workflow. Thanks for watching!
